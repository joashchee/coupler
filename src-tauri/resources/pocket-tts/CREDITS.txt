Pocket TTS: the speech model and voices in this folder
=======================================================

Coupler speaks some characters' lines with Pocket TTS, by Kyutai
(kyutai.org). Everything here is used as published except where noted.

The model (model.safetensors, tokenizer.model)
  Pocket TTS, by Manu Orsini, Simon Rouard, Gabriel De Marmiesse,
  Vaclav Volhejn, Neil Zeghidour and Alexandre Defossez (Kyutai),
  from huggingface.co/kyutai/pocket-tts-without-voice-cloning,
  licensed under CC BY 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Changed by Coupler: the parts that make a voice from a recording (the
  Mimi encoder, its transformer, the downsampler and the speaker
  projection) are left out. Coupler offers these voices only, and
  never copies anyone's voice.

  Kyutai's terms for the model forbid, among other things, voice
  impersonation or cloning without explicit and lawful consent, and
  presenting generated speech as a genuine recording of a real person.
